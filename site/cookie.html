Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file that is sent to your computer or other internet-connected device when you visit a website. Cookies help us recognize your browser and can be used to store information and settings within your browser.

We and our third-party service providers may use the following cookies and other similar tracking technologies:

● Cookies: Small text files used to store your preferences and settings.

● SDKs (Software Development Kits): Code embedded in an application to collect device information and interaction data.

● Pixel Tags / Web Beacons: Transparent images used to track page visits and ad effectiveness.

Cookie Categories

Our website uses cookies to provide a personalized user experience and improve site performance. First-party cookies are set directly by our website to store your activities and preferences on the site, while third-party cookies are set by other websites or service providers. These may be used to track your activity across different sites for purposes like ad targeting and behavioral analysis.

We have categorized these cookies based on the guidelines from the ICC UK Cookie Guide.

Category 1: Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the basic functionality of the website. They are typically used to respond to user actions like logging in or filling out forms. Without these cookies, the website will not function properly.

Category 2: Performance Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how users interact with the website, such as which pages they visit, how long they stay, and any errors they encounter. This data is used solely to improve the website's performance and user experience.

Category 3: Functionality Cookies

These cookies are used to remember your choices and preferences (like your username, language, or region) to provide enhanced, personalized features and content. They improve your overall browsing experience.

Category 4: Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver more relevant advertising content and to measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns. They can also limit the number of times you see an ad and help advertisers understand your interests to provide a more personalized ad experience.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage your preferences in two ways. Please note that disabling, blocking, or deleting cookies in your settings may affect your ability to fully use our website's features, and some services may not function properly.

1. Using the Cookie Banner on Our Website

When you first visit our website, you will see a Cookie management banner. This banner allows you to adjust your preferences, such as accepting or rejecting certain types of cookies, like marketing or performance analytics cookies.

2. Through Your Browser Settings

You can choose to manage cookies in your browser's settings. Most browsers allow you to be warned each time a cookie is set, or you can choose to disable all cookies entirely.
